# Product Mission

> Last Updated: 2026-03-11
> Version: 1.0.0

## Pitch

CallerPH is an AI-powered voice agent service that helps Philippine small businesses stop losing customers to missed calls by providing an affordable, 24/7 AI receptionist that answers in natural English and Tagalog — at a fraction of the cost of hiring staff or using enterprise solutions.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Solo Operators & Micro-Businesses:** Service providers (groomers, repair shops, freelancers) who can't answer calls while working
- **Small Clinics & Professional Offices:** Dental clinics, law offices, accounting firms with small staff who miss calls during consultations
- **Retail & Hospitality SMEs:** Restaurants, salons, spas, florists overwhelmed during peak hours

### User Personas

- **Context:** Drives to client homes all day, phone rings while mid-groom
- **Pain Points:** Misses 5-10 calls daily, loses new customers to competitors, can't afford a receptionist
- **Goals:** Capture every lead, look professional, grow through referrals

**Dr. Santos** (40-55 years old)
- **Role:** Owner of a small dental clinic with 2 staff
- **Context:** Staff are busy with patients, phones go unanswered during peak hours and after 5 PM
- **Pain Points:** Lost revenue from missed appointment inquiries, patients going to competitors, paying for an answering service that costs ₱15,000+/mo
- **Goals:** Never miss a patient inquiry, reduce admin overhead, maintain professional image

**Aron** (30-40 years old)
- **Role:** Digital service provider (landing pages, web design)
- **Context:** Runs PagePro, a landing page service for Filipino entrepreneurs. Gets inquiries via FB and website but misses calls while building client work

- **Goals:** Add phone as a lead channel, automate initial qualification, respond to every inquiry instantly

## The Problem

### Missed Calls = Missed Revenue

62% of calls to small businesses go unanswered, and 85% of callers who don't reach someone will never call back. For a Filipino SME earning ₱50,000-200,000/month, even 5 missed leads per week can mean ₱20,000-80,000 in lost monthly revenue.

**Our Solution:** An AI voice agent that answers every call instantly, collects caller information, explains services, and ensures no lead is lost.

### Existing Solutions Are Too Expensive

Philippine AI chatbot agencies charge ₱50,000 build fees and ₱25,000-45,000/month retainers. Hiring a dedicated receptionist costs ₱12,000-18,000/month plus benefits. These are out of reach for micro and small businesses.

**Our Solution:** CallerPH starts at ₱5,000-15,000 setup with ₱2,500-8,000/month retainer — 80% cheaper than alternatives.

### Language Barrier in AI Tools

Most AI voice solutions are English-only or don't handle Taglish (mixed Tagalog-English) naturally. Filipino callers speak in Taglish by default, and robotic English-only responses feel impersonal.

**Our Solution:** Powered by Gemini Native Audio, CallerPH understands and responds in English, Tagalog, and Taglish naturally.

## Differentiators

### 10x Cheaper Than Competition

Unlike Bots at Work (₱50K build + ₱25K/mo) and enterprise solutions, CallerPH uses free-tier AI (Gemini) and minimal infrastructure to deliver the same core value at ₱5K-15K setup + ₱3K-8K/mo. Small businesses can actually afford this.

### Voice-First, Not Chat-First

Unlike most Philippine AI agencies that focus on Messenger/website chatbots, CallerPH is built for phone calls — where the real missed opportunities happen. A ringing phone with no answer loses more customers than an unanswered chat message.

### Natural Taglish Conversations

Unlike foreign platforms (VoiceGenie, Synthflow) that bolt on Filipino language support, CallerPH uses Gemini 2.5 Flash Native Audio which natively understands tone, emotion, and language-switching — delivering conversations that feel like talking to a real Filipino receptionist.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **24/7 AI Voice Receptionist:** Answers every call instantly, any time of day or night
- **Natural Taglish Support:** Speaks and understands English, Tagalog, and mixed Taglish naturally
- **Lead Capture:** Collects caller name, contact number, inquiry type, and preferred callback time
- **Business-Specific Responses:** Custom system prompts per client covering services, pricing, hours, and FAQs
- **Call Logging:** Records every interaction with caller details and conversation summary

### Client Management Features

- **Per-Client Customization:** Only the system prompt changes per client — same codebase, infinite deployments
- **Client Dashboard:** Web interface for business owners to view call logs, leads, and conversation summaries
- **Call Summary Notifications:** SMS or email alerts to business owner after each call with lead details

### Business Growth Features

- **Referral-Ready Architecture:** Each new client deployment takes hours, not days — scalable service model
- **Demo Mode:** Live demo capability to pitch new clients on the spot
- **Multi-Client Management:** Single admin dashboard to manage all deployed voice agents
